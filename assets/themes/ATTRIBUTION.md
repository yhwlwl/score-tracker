# Background sources

Graph Paper and Polka Dots are adapted from **Hero Patterns** by **Steve Schoger**.
Source: https://heropatterns.com/ (pattern SVGs in https://heropatterns.com/js/app.js)
License: **CC BY 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: recolored, reduced opacity and dot size; simplified and respaced the grid.

Cloud waves and soft gradients are original CSS/SVG compositions.

## Stickers

The 24 original SVG line ornaments are used. The experimental 3D stickers have been removed.

All wallpaper and sticker assets are served from this site. The app makes no runtime requests to Unsplash or GitHub.
The AI-generated concept wallpapers explored during design are not included in this catalog.
